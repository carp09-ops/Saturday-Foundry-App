# Beta 8 — Graphite and crimson artwork

The native preview now uses a shared visual language: charcoal/graphite surfaces, restrained crimson light and borders, neutral metal for everyday tools, and gold for honors. Official school logos and the original Foundry branding remain unchanged. Original coach-card pixels, portraits and lettering are preserved; the cards now sit inside consistent graphite/crimson display frames.

Distinct art roles replace repeated helmet imagery:

- Account: silver whistle and crimson lanyard (`assets/graphite/art/account-whistle.webp`).
- Coach without an original card, or a failed original-card request: sideline headset (`assets/graphite/art/coach-headset.webp`).
- League chooser and small story thumbnails: stadium (`assets/graphite/art/league-stadium.webp`).
- Legacy banner: gold football trophy in a graphite gallery (`assets/graphite/art/legacy-room.webp`).
- Missing school logo: existing neutral helmet, with the school name still provided in text.

Original transparent trophy artwork is preserved and displayed on graphite backgrounds with restrained gold framing. Trophy labels and recorded honors remain data-driven. Existing stadium/tunnel story and login photography remains in place.

All four new assets were made with the built-in image-generation tool and encoded to WebP at the generated resolution. No generated letters, initials or substitute school logos. No app permissions, league data or database logic changes. Advanced forms continue to use the retained runtime; this pass targets the native site's artwork.
